CHINA

麻婆豆腐

Mapo tofu

25 MIN2 SERVINGS
↻

FROM OUR RECIPE JOURNAL

Mapo tofu

What you need

  • 400 g firm tofu, cubed
  • 100 g ground pork
  • 1 tbsp doubanjiang
  • 1 tsp Sichuan pepper
  • 150 ml stock; garlic; scallion

Let’s make it

  1. Gently simmer tofu cubes in water; drain.
  2. Brown pork with minced garlic, then stir in doubanjiang.
  3. Add stock and tofu. Simmer gently for 5–7 minutes.
  4. Thicken with a little cornstarch slurry; finish with Sichuan pepper and scallion.

A simplified home-kitchen version. Season to your taste.

SOUTH KOREA

비빔밥

Korean mixed rice

30 MIN2 SERVINGS
↻

FROM OUR RECIPE JOURNAL

Korean mixed rice

What you need

  • 2 bowls cooked rice
  • 1 carrot; spinach; bean sprouts
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 tbsp gochujang
  • Sesame oil and sesame seeds

Let’s make it

  1. Slice carrot. Cook each vegetable separately with a little oil.
  2. Divide hot rice between two bowls.
  3. Arrange vegetables over rice and top each bowl with a fried egg.
  4. Add gochujang and sesame oil; mix together before eating.

A simplified home-kitchen version. Season to your taste.

JAPAN

味噌汁

Miso soup

15 MIN2 SERVINGS
↻

FROM OUR RECIPE JOURNAL

Miso soup

What you need

  • 500 ml dashi stock
  • 150 g silken tofu
  • 2 tbsp miso paste
  • 1 tbsp dried wakame
  • 1 scallion, sliced

Let’s make it

  1. Soak wakame and cut tofu into small cubes.
  2. Bring dashi to a gentle simmer; add tofu and wakame.
  3. Turn heat off. Dissolve miso in a ladle of warm broth, then stir it in.
  4. Top with scallion. Serve warm without boiling the miso.

A simplified home-kitchen version. Season to your taste.

THAILAND

ต้มยำกุ้ง

Tom yum shrimp soup

25 MIN2 SERVINGS
↻

FROM OUR RECIPE JOURNAL

Tom yum shrimp soup

What you need

  • 250 g peeled shrimp
  • 600 ml stock; 150 g mushrooms
  • 2 lemongrass stalks; 3 slices galangal
  • 3 makrut lime leaves; chili to taste
  • Fish sauce and lime juice, to taste

Let’s make it

  1. Bruise lemongrass. Simmer with stock, galangal and lime leaves for 8 minutes.
  2. Add mushrooms and chili; simmer until mushrooms soften.
  3. Add shrimp and cook until opaque throughout.
  4. Turn off heat, season with fish sauce and lime juice; remove tough aromatics before eating.

A simplified home-kitchen version. Season to your taste.

VIETNAM

Gỏi cuốn

Fresh spring rolls

25 MIN2 SERVINGS
↻

FROM OUR RECIPE JOURNAL

Fresh spring rolls

What you need

  • 6 rice-paper wrappers
  • 100 g cooked rice vermicelli
  • 12 cooked shrimp halves
  • Lettuce, mint and cucumber
  • Hoisin dipping sauce

Let’s make it

  1. Prepare cooked noodles, cooked shrimp and sliced vegetables.
  2. Dip one wrapper briefly into warm water and lay flat.
  3. Add lettuce, noodles, herbs and shrimp to the lower third.
  4. Fold sides inward and roll snugly. Repeat; serve with dipping sauce.

A simplified home-kitchen version. Season to your taste.

MALAYSIA

Nasi lemak

Coconut rice

35 MIN2 SERVINGS
↻

FROM OUR RECIPE JOURNAL

Coconut rice

What you need

  • 1 cup rice
  • 200 ml coconut milk; water as needed
  • 1 pandan leaf (optional)
  • 2 boiled eggs; sliced cucumber
  • Roasted peanuts and prepared sambal

Let’s make it

  1. Rinse rice. Add coconut milk and enough water for your rice cooker’s usual liquid level.
  2. Add pandan if using; cook until rice is tender.
  3. Prepare boiled eggs, cucumber and roasted peanuts.
  4. Serve coconut rice with the toppings and a spoonful of sambal.

A simplified home-kitchen version. Season to your taste.

INDONESIA

Nasi goreng

Indonesian fried rice

20 MIN2 SERVINGS
↻

FROM OUR RECIPE JOURNAL

Indonesian fried rice

What you need

  • 2 bowls chilled cooked rice
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 garlic cloves; 1 shallot
  • 1–2 tbsp kecap manis
  • Oil; scallion; cucumber

Let’s make it

  1. Finely chop garlic and shallot. Fry in a little oil until fragrant.
  2. Add rice and stir-fry until piping hot.
  3. Stir in kecap manis and sliced scallion.
  4. Top with fried eggs and serve with cucumber.

A simplified home-kitchen version. Season to your taste.

PHILIPPINES

Adobong manok

Chicken adobo

45 MIN2 SERVINGS
↻

FROM OUR RECIPE JOURNAL

Chicken adobo

What you need

  • 500 g chicken thighs
  • 60 ml soy sauce; 60 ml vinegar
  • 120 ml water
  • 4 garlic cloves; 2 bay leaves
  • 1 tsp whole peppercorns

Let’s make it

  1. Brown chicken in a little oil.
  2. Add crushed garlic, soy sauce, vinegar, water, bay leaves and peppercorns.
  3. Bring to a simmer; cover and cook until chicken is cooked through and tender.
  4. Uncover and reduce sauce slightly. Serve with rice.

A simplified home-kitchen version. Season to your taste.
